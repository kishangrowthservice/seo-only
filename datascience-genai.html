Your 12 Month Roadmap to Becoming a
Data + GenAI Professional

Months 1-2

Core Programming & Data
Handling

  • Learn Python from scratch with real-world datasets and logic-building exercises  
  • Use Excel for quick data manipulation, visualization, and analysis 
  • Query and manage structured databases using SQL with hands-on assignments 

Months 3-4

Statistical Thinking & Data Analysis

  • Understand statistical concepts essential for data-driven decisions 
  • Conduct Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) to identify trends, anomalies, and relationships  
  • Work on data cleaning pipelines and summary reporting techniques 

Months 5-6

Data Visualization & Business Intelligence

  • Create data visualizations using Matplotlib and Seaborn 
  • Build interactive dashboards with Power BI to convey business insights  
  • Apply storytelling techniques to present findings to technical and non-technical teams  

Months 7-8

Machine Learning Foundations

  • Train supervised and unsupervised models using Scikit-learn  
  • Understand model evaluation metrics and tuning techniques 
  • Develop classification, regression, and clustering projects with real datasets 

Months 9-10

Generative AI Integration & Automation

  • Work with Hugging Face Transformers, LangChain, and OpenAI APIs  
  • Use LLMs to build GenAI-based tools such as chatbots, text summarizers, and content generators 
  • Automate repetitive data science tasks using AI agents and zero-code platforms 

Months 11-12

Machine Learning Foundations

  • Design a comprehensive project combining ML and GenAI for real-world business use cases  
  • Participate in mock interviews, personalized resume sessions, and domain-specific mentoring  
  • Build a public portfolio on GitHub featuring notebooks, dashboards, and end-to-end pipelines

FAQ's

Do I need any prior coding or AI experience to join?

No prior experience required. We start from Python fundamentals and progressively cover advanced AI and generative AI concepts

Will I receive a certification after completing the course?

Yes, you’ll earn an industry-recognized certificate co-branded with IBM, validating your skills in data science and generative AI.

Does the course include placement support?

Absolutely. We offer mock interviews, personalized resume reviews, and connections with hiring partners.

Are the classes live or pre-recorded?

We provide live instructor-led sessions with interactive Q&A, plus access to recorded content for flexible learning

How long does the course take to complete?

The program runs for approximately 6 months, with part-time options available depending on the batch.

What kind of projects will I work on?

Hands-on projects include predictive analytics, building AI chatbots, automated report generation, and deploying generative AI models.

Is this course suitable for someone from a non-technical background?

Yes, it’s designed for beginners and professionals from any background who want to pivot into data science and AI.

What job roles can I pursue after completing this course?

Roles include Data Scientist, AI Engineer, Prompt Engineer, Generative AI Specialist, Business Intelligence Developer, and more.

Will I learn how to work with popular generative AI frameworks and tools?

Yes, the curriculum covers leading frameworks like OpenAI GPT, Hugging Face Transformers, and practical AI toolkits.

Can I access course materials after completion?

Yes, you get lifetime access to all course content and updates.

Is financial assistance or installment payment available?

Yes, flexible payment plans and scholarships are available for eligible candidates.
